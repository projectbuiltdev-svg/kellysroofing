---
name: Page introduction format
description: Approved content structure for homepage, service-page, and location-page introductions.
---

Use short, separated paragraphs for the company overview, service history and range, joined-up interior work, mission, and free-quote call to action. Place Contact and WhatsApp actions directly below.

For location pages, preserve the structure but vary the heading and locally important wording; include authentic built-environment or geographic context rather than swapping only the place name.

**Why:** The user approved this format for easier scanning and explicitly wants location pages to avoid duplicated copy.

**How to apply:** Reuse the hierarchy and verified company facts across public landing pages, while writing unique service- or locality-specific lead copy.
